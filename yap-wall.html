Before you yap

Keep it kind, relevant, and respectful.

Please do not share private information about yourself or anyone else, including phone numbers, addresses, workplaces, medical details, or identifying details about someone who has not agreed to be named.

Every submission is reviewed before publication. We may edit for clarity and length, publish anonymously, decline, or remove submissions that are harmful, defamatory, discriminatory, spammy, off-topic, or disclose someone's private information.

Bring a thought to the wall

Start with a prompt.

  • What is a friendship lesson you learned the hard way?
  • What is one relationship ick that signals a deeper value?
  • What is your current “maybe the problem is me” realization?
  • What is a money conversation people avoid having?
  • What topic should Kerry yap about next?
  • What is one thing you wish you knew in your early twenties?
  • What is your unpopular but respectful take on dating, culture, or ambition?
  • What helps you stay grounded when social media becomes too loud?
  • What is a red flag people excuse too easily?
